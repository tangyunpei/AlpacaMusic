### Local data and network requests

The following describes the current repository implementation; it does not cover user-modified versions or custom services:

- NetEase Cloud Music and QQ Music logins take place in a separate WebKit environment. Login information is not imported from Safari, Chrome, or other applications. Once connected, the application reads the platform session needed for this authorization to validate the account and make subsequent requests.
- Saved NetEase Cloud Music and QQ Music sessions and Spotify tokens use the local Keychain. These items are not configured for iCloud synchronization. Apple Music authorization is managed by the system and MusicKit. Do not publish cookies, tokens, signing configurations, private keys, complete authentication requests, or logs containing them.
- The music library, playlists, favorites, playback position, lyrics files, and some caches are stored locally. Signing out of a music source does not automatically delete imported track information or playlists. To revoke authorization on the platform side, use the channels provided by that platform or the system.
- Using online music sources, artwork, web logins, or lyrics queries sends corresponding network requests to the relevant platforms and their content services. The project is not fully offline software and cannot promise that third-party services do not record request information.
- Automatic lyrics matching for Apple Music is currently enabled by default and can be disabled in the lyrics menu. LRCLIB queries send the song title, artist, album, and track duration. The application does not attach platform login cookies or Spotify tokens to these queries. LRCLIB results may not match the current recording version, and character-by-character or word-by-word timing derived from line-level lyrics may be estimated.
- Users configure custom services and decide whether to trust them. Their operators are responsible for their data-handling rules. Login credentials for native music sources are not automatically forwarded to a custom service when a direct connection fails.
