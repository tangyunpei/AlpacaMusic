Loading or calling these resources does not make them open-source assets of this project or establish authorization for an official third-party platform partnership.
- **Music platforms and LRCLIB**: APIs, music, lyrics, cover art, and other content returned by Apple Music, Spotify, NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, Soda Music, and [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net/docs) are subject to their respective rights and service terms. The repository does not directly include LRCLIB server code; the license of that code does not establish authorization for all lyrics.
- **Content generated by the project**: [DemoLibrary.swift](Sources/AlpacaMusic/DemoLibrary.swift) generates preview audio and cover art. These are separate from commercial songs and album images returned by platforms. Original code, documentation, and project-owned resources that the project has the right to license are provided under the project's [LICENSE](LICENSE), except for system components and third-party marks.
- **Application icon provenance**: [AppIcon-Bauhaus.png](Resources/Brand/AppIcon-Bauhaus.png) is the alpaca icon source image generated through the built-in imagegen tool. Its prompt is recorded in [app-icon-prompt.md](Resources/Brand/app-icon-prompt.md). [make-icon.swift](scripts/make-icon.swift) reads this PNG and generates icons at each required size; the build script then packages them as ICNS. The source image and alternative dog version are documented in the [brand icon notes](Resources/Brand/README.md).
