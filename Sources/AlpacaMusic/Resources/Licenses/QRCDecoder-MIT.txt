QQ QRC format interoperability

QQWordLyricCodec.swift is a Swift adaptation of the algorithm and tables in
qrc-decoder by apoint123, which attributes its original implementation to
LyricDecoder by SuJiKiNen. It is used solely for lyrics-format interoperability;
it is not a cryptographic facility for protecting accounts or application data.

Sources:
https://github.com/apoint123/qrc-decoder
https://github.com/SuJiKiNen/LyricDecoder
